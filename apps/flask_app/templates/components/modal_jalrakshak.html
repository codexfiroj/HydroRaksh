JalSuraksha: Collaborative Filtering & Latent Topic Modeling for Groundwater Intelligence

Engineered by Mohd Shami (B.Tech Data Science). This platform integrates physics-guided hydrogeological modeling, spatial collaborative filtering (HG-CF), and unsupervised natural language topic extraction (LDA) across 700+ administrative districts in India.

Backend Architecture: Python-first Flask + SQLAlchemy ORM + ML Models
Research Workspace: 16 Reproducible Jupyter Notebooks (00_ to 15_)
Primary Reference Basin: Uttar Pradesh (75 calibrated districts)
Data Compliance: Government Open Data License (GODL India)
